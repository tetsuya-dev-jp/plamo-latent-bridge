# Upstream models and evaluation data

The Python code and generated synthetic question templates in this repository
use the project's MIT license. Upstream models and external evaluation data
retain their own terms.

- **PLaMo 3 Translate 0.8B**, Preferred Networks: [model card](https://huggingface.co/pfnet/plamo-3-translate-0.8b),
  [PLaMo Community License](https://plamo.preferredai.jp/info/plamo-community-license-en).
  The repository downloads the pinned model through Hugging Face and does not
  redistribute its weights or custom implementation files.
- **Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct**, Qwen: [model card and Apache 2.0 license](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct).
  The pretrained weights are downloaded separately.
- **Multilingual Grade School Math (MGSM)**, Freda Shi and colleagues:
  [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03057),
  [official Japanese data](https://github.com/google-research/url-nlp/blob/452a21ad3dae5668c06ceeac21ff073e1e40f9be/mgsm/mgsm_ja.tsv),
  [CC BY 4.0 license](https://github.com/google-research/url-nlp/blob/452a21ad3dae5668c06ceeac21ff073e1e40f9be/mgsm/LICENSE).
  MGSM translations generated by PLaMo are adaptations of those questions and
  are identified in `data/translations_mgsm.jsonl`. They retain attribution to
  MGSM and are not official benchmark translations or reference solutions.

The learned hidden-state interface follows the general model-bridging approach
of Dongkeun Yoon and colleagues' [LangBridge paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.405/).
This project implements its own adaptation rather than copying the LangBridge
codebase. Experimental implementation was produced with DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and
reviewed through tests, model smoke runs and the published result records.
